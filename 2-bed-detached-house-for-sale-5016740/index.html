Steeped in history, this exceptional property – originally built over a century ago as a traditional Forge – seamlessly blends character with modern elegance. Tucked away on a remarkably private site, this home has been meticulously upgraded in recent years to a modern bespoke finish. Offering the rare combination of rich heritage, secluded living and contemporary sophistication, this unique home is a true sanctuary. The accommodation extends to approx. 117 sq.m. and comprises entrance hall, living room, two bedrooms, bathroom, kitchen, utility room and sunroom. ‘Rothby Forge’ is conveniently located with all amenities on your doorstep including Dublin Airport, Swords Main Street, M1 & M50 motorways. Viewing is highly recommended to experience the charm, seclusion and craftmanship of this home.
